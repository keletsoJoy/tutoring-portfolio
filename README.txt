SYSTEM SOFTWARE 1A – TUTOR E-PORTFOLIO keletso
==========================================

Files:
- index.html              Main portfolio website
- css/style.css           Custom visual styling
- js/script.js            Navigation, animations and interactive behaviour
- assets/                 Place your photos/screenshots/documents here

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
1. Keep the folder structure unchanged.
2. Double-click index.html, or right-click it and open with a browser.
3. The site is responsive and can be viewed on desktop, tablet or mobile.

BEFORE SUBMISSION
-----------------
Replace every item shown in square brackets [LIKE THIS] with your own information.

At minimum, update:
- Your full name
- Student number
- Your photograph
- Personal statement
- Your actual tutoring journey
- Training and development activities
- Tutorial dates/activities
- Technology you personally used
- Reflection entries
- Real evidence/photos/screenshots
- Authentic student feedback
- Feedback percentages, if you collected structured feedback

IMPORTANT
---------
Do not submit invented student feedback or evidence. Replace placeholders only with genuine
information and evidence you are permitted to share.
