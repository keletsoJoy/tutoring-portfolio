MODULE OVERVIEW

System Software 1 A

Module code: SS15W0A/00/2026

The module is designed to equip learners with basic technical skills in the information technology environment, with emphasis on number systems, basic hardware and software concepts, and the fundamentals of computer networks.

NQF Level 5 20 Credits 200 Notional Hours

Learning Outcomes

  • Convert between decimal, binary, octal and hexadecimal systems.
  • Perform binary arithmetic and work with signed numbers.
  • Explain logic gates using symbols and truth tables.
  • Select, install and configure computer components.
  • Apply preventive maintenance and troubleshooting processes.
  • Explain how computers communicate on networks.

I attended a two-day Peer-to-Peer Facilitation Training focused on developing effective and student-centred tutoring practices. The training strengthened my understanding of the tutor’s role as a facilitator, motivator and role model rather than simply delivering content.

06 • REFLECTION

Reflections & Lessons Learnt

Key lessons, challenges and experiences that shaped my growth and development as a tutor.

Reflection 01

What worked well?

Reflection 01

The use of examples involving binary questions was particularly effective because it enabled students to develop a clearer understanding of the concepts than a purely theoretical explanation would have provided. Demonstrating the practical application of binary questions increased student participation and created opportunities for learners to apply their knowledge. It also helped them recognise how these concepts may be used in authentic contexts, thereby strengthening the connection between theoretical principles and practical application.

Reflection 02

What was challenging?

Reflection 02

Teaching the theoretical content was challenging because it required me to explain technical concepts in a clear and accessible manner without compromising the accuracy or completeness of the information. I therefore selected my language and examples carefully to ensure that students could follow the explanations while developing a precise understanding of the subject matter. This experience reinforced the importance of presenting complex ideas in manageable stages, using relevant and clearly structured examples, and checking students’ understanding regularly throughout the lesson.

Reflection 03

What did I learn?

Reflection 03

I learnt that effective tutoring requires patience, preparation and good communication. I also learnt that knowing a subject is not enough. A tutor must be able to communicate knowledge in a way that students can understand..

Reflection 04

What will I improve?

Reflection 04

In future tutorials, I would like to:

  • Use more interactive activities.
  • Provide additional examples.
  • Encourage greater student participation.
  • Improve time management.
  • Make greater use of visual learning resources.
  • Collect feedback more regularly.
  • Adapt explanations according to students’ needs.
